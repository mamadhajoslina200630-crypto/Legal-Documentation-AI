2. Indian Legal services — YES, you need legal data access

This is the important part.

Your Indian Legal Context Assistant, Judgment Summarization, and Legal Search need reliable legal sources.

You have three choices:

Option A — Internet/API based

Use external legal sources/APIs and retrieve information when required.

For example, Indian Kanoon provides an API for searching and retrieving Indian legal documents, with document, search, metadata and fragment endpoints. Their terms also require attribution when their results/documents are used.

This is enough for your first working version.

Option B — Public datasets

You can later maintain your own legal knowledge database.

There are already public datasets available. For example, the KanoonGPT Indian Case Laws dataset currently covers Supreme Court + 25 High Courts and is released under Apache-2.0.

There is also an Indian Legal Documents dataset containing Acts, Rules, Notifications, Orders, Regulations, etc.

There is also a public AWS dataset containing Supreme Court judgments from 1950–2025, including regional-language versions.

So you don't need to manually collect thousands of PDFs yourself.
